Hi, I'm Chen. I'm doing my PhD in Information Science at Nanyang Technological University with Prof. Dion Goh. I'm curious about how people make sense of misinformation and synthetic media, and how conversational AI and LLM tutors can make learning a little easier.

Before the PhD, I studied Data Science and Machine Learning at University College London, where my master's dissertation was supervised by Prof. John Shawe-Taylor. Earlier, I completed bachelor's degrees in Applied Statistics and Journalism at the Communication University of China. Both my master's and undergraduate dissertations explored financial markets and stock price forecasting. This interdisciplinary background has left me curious about a wide range of questions across technology, media, finance, and human behavior. I am comfortable using both computational and qualitative methods, depending on what the question calls for. Before returning to academia, I spent four years working as a data scientist in industry.
